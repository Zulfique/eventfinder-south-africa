## Full description (max 4000 characters)

```
EventFinder South Africa helps you find what is happening near you, save the events you care
about, and organise your own — whether you are in Cape Town for the weekend or planning a
night out in Johannesburg.

FIND EVENTS NEAR YOU
• Browse by category — music, concerts, festivals, nightlife, food, sports, arts, comedy,
  business, networking, technology, education, family, markets, outdoors, travel, charity,
  theatre, movies, fashion, cars, wellness, religion and community.
• Search by name, venue or vibe.
• Set your city and a search radius, and sort by what is happening soon.
• Open an event to see the date, venue, organiser, attendee count and a map of where it is.

CREATE AND MANAGE EVENTS
• Publish your own event with a title, description, category, venue, date range and cover image.
• Edit or delete events you created.
• Scan tickets with the built-in QR scanner.
• Keep an eye on who is attending.

PLAN WITH FRIENDS
• Save favourites and build collections of events you want to go to.
• Use group planning to agree on a meeting point and a proposed time.
• Set reminders so you do not miss the start.

BUILT FOR SOUTH AFRICA
• Available in English and Afrikaans.
• Light and dark themes, with an optional Material You colour mode.
• Guest mode — browse and save favourites without creating an account.
• Sign in with email or with Google, and optionally unlock the app with your fingerprint.
• Report suspicious events or organisers straight from the event screen.

YOUR DATA, YOUR CHOICE
• Location permission is optional; type your city and the app works just as well.
• Notifications are optional and can be turned off at any time.
• Delete your account and its data from Settings whenever you want.
• No advertising SDK, no advertising ID, and we do not sell your data.

EventFinder South Africa is an independent app for discovering and organising events. Event
details, including dates and venues, are supplied by organisers and attendees — always confirm
with the organiser before travelling.
```
