Privacy Policy

EventFinder South Africa · Android app com.eventfinder.app · Last updated 6 November 2026

EventFinder South Africa (“EventFinder”, “we”) helps people find, create and plan events around South Africa. This policy explains what the Android app collects, why, and the choices you have. It describes the behaviour of the released app; it is not a description of any other website or service.

1. Information we handle

WhatWhenWhy
Account details: name, email address, password (stored only as a hash), preferred language, profile photo When you create an account or sign in with email or Google To create and secure your account and personalise the app
Content you create: events, tickets, collections, favourites, reviews, reports, group-plan messages When you use those features To show your content to you and, for public events, to other people
Location: the city and search radius you choose, and your device’s approximate location if you grant the permission When you set a city or ask for nearby events To show events near you. The app works without location permission using the city you type
Notification token and reminder preferences When you allow notifications or set a reminder To deliver event reminders and updates
Guest profile: the name “Guest”, an empty email and a locally generated identifier When you tap “Continue as Guest” So you can browse and save favourites without an account

We do not collect your contacts, calendar, SMS, call logs, photos library or precise background location. There is no advertising SDK and no advertising identifier in the app, and we do not sell personal information.

2. Storage on your device

Your account mirror, favourites, collections, drafts and preferences are stored in a local database on your phone. Signing out or deleting the app removes this local copy. Android’s own backup is switched off for this app (allowBackup=false), so your data is not copied into a cloud backup by the operating system.

3. Service providers

  • Firebase (Google) — authentication, the events database and notification delivery. Google processes this data on our behalf.
  • Google Sign-In — only if you choose “Continue with Google”. We receive your name, email address and Google account identifier.
  • Our API server — account, event and ticket records, hosted on cloud infrastructure.
  • Unsplash and Pixabay — event cover images. Loading an image sends your IP address and app user-agent to that provider.
  • OpenStreetMap — map tiles for the map view, which similarly receive your IP address.

4. Analytics

Settings includes an opt-in “anonymous usage analytics” switch. The released app does not bundle a third-party analytics SDK, so nothing is shared with an analytics provider today. If that changes, the switch will control it and this policy will be updated first.

5. Your choices and rights

  • Delete your account — Settings → Delete account removes your account and its server-side account data and clears the local copy on the device.
  • Data access or correction — most details can be edited in the app; otherwise contact us at the address below.
  • Permissions — location and notifications are optional and can be revoked in Android settings at any time. The app keeps working without them.
  • Guest mode — no account is created, and nothing is uploaded until you create content or sign in.

6. Children

The app is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them. If you believe a child has provided information, contact us and we will remove it.

7. Security and retention

Traffic between the app and our services is encrypted with HTTPS, and cleartext HTTP is disabled in the app. Account data is kept while your account exists; deleting the account removes it. Content you posted publicly, such as a public event, may remain visible for a short period while caches expire.

8. Changes

If this policy changes materially we will update this page and the date above before the change takes effect in a released version of the app.

9. Contact

Questions or requests about your data: privacy@eventfinder.co.za.
